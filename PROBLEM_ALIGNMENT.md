# Problem Alignment — AirNest

## Core Problem
Urban families and community groups (such as RWAs) face fragmented air quality readings without clear, personalized guidance on daily routines, indoor protection, and low-cost sustainable alternatives.

## Target Users & Modes
- **Household Mode:** Tailored around children, older adults, respiratory sensitivity, cooking fuel, air purifiers, and commute patterns.
- **Community Mode:** Scales guidance for neighborhood groups and residential associations.

## How AirNest Addresses It
1. **Actionable Daily Decisions:** Translates AQI/PM2.5/PM10/ozone snapshots into clear outdoor/indoor timing and window/ventilation advice.
2. **Deterministic + AI-Assisted Planning:** Generates a deterministic baseline plan immediately and supports optional AI plan refinement within strict schema and numeric guardrails.
3. **Measurable Habit & Exposure Impact:** Tracks daily checklist actions, estimated exposure reduction, XP/streaks, and local persistence with offline fallback.
